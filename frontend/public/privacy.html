Yosuu workbench

Privacy

Files and links are sent to the server running this instance. When link processing is enabled, that server also contacts the selected platform to fetch the media.

Yosuu keeps uploads and exports in temporary storage. It schedules them for deletion 15 minutes after the last conversion starts or the export finishes, and clears its temporary storage on normal shutdown.

Yosuu does not include analytics. Operators may have their own logging, hosting, and privacy policies.
